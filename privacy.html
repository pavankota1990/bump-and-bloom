Privacy Policy — Bump & Bloom

Last updated: October 19, 2026

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Bump & Bloom does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — including names, due dates, meals, symptoms, visits, and logs — is stored exclusively on your device and never sent to any server, third party, or cloud service controlled by us.

ICLOUD SYNC AND BACKUP

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your app data may be synced or backed up to your own iCloud account. This happens within your personal Apple account, is controlled by your device settings and Apple's privacy policy, and is never accessible to us. We do not receive or have access to this data.

NOTIFICATIONS

The app schedules local notifications on your device for meal reminders, water reminders, and appointment alerts. These notifications are processed entirely on-device and no notification content is sent to any external server.

NO ANALYTICS OR TRACKING

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

Bump & Bloom requires no account, login, or registration of any kind.

CHILDREN

This app is not directed at children under 13.
