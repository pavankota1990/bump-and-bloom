FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is my data backed up automatically?

Yes. Bump & Bloom saves your data to your device continuously. If you have iCloud enabled on your iPhone, your data syncs automatically to your own iCloud account. You can also manually export a backup file from the Plan tab at any time.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Plan → Export my data and save or share the backup file. On your new phone, go to Plan → Import from backup and paste the exported text.

Why aren't my reminders working?

Check that notifications are allowed: go to Plan → Reminders → Notifications. If it says "Off", tap Allow. Also ensure Do Not Disturb is not enabled and the app has not been force-closed.

How do I change my due date?

Go to the Plan tab → About section → Due date.

Can I delete my data?

All data is on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app.
